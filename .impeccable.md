## Design Context

### Users
Personal and student task tracking. Users need to capture assignments, see deadlines, and move work forward without a complex project-management setup.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, simple, approachable. The interface should feel like an organized study desk and make progress feel manageable.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light interface with warm paper neutrals, muted forest green, a small terracotta accent, generous space, and readable type. Use a calm task list, clear filters, and a side panel for task details and editing. Avoid enterprise dashboard clutter.

### Design Principles
- Make the next action clear and keep everyday controls visible.
- Use concise, encouraging language without distracting from the task.
- Combine text labels with color for priority and status.
- Keep all task actions available on small screens and through the keyboard.
- Respect reduced motion and preserve user input on errors.
